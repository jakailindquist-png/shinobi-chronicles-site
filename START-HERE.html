Your first mission

01

Import the pack

Extract the Windows ZIP. In Prism Launcher, choose Add Instance → Import, then the enclosed .mrpack. Use your Minecraft Java account and 64-bit Java 17. The launcher fetches 27 pinned files, about 292 MiB, plus Minecraft and Forge.

02

Meet your character

Create a normal Survival world on Normal difficulty. Complete Ninshu's native introduction and read its Ninja Guide. Try 6–8 GB game memory if your PC has enough RAM; this is an unbenchmarked starting point. Shaders ship off.

03

Enter the shinobi world

Use /story to choose your mission path, then /story embark. Follow the journal, meet your contact, and set a bed. Use /shinobi for equipment and beast dossiers, and /shinobi mentors for native teachers.

A world with continuity

Villages & landmarks

Five major villages, minor nations, examination grounds, hideouts, sage regions, the Valley of the End, and an original beast sanctuary. Fixed coordinates, distinct architecture, and interiors.

Missions & relationships

Chronological story retellings, five village allegiances and an alternate rogue path. Persistent contacts, trust, choices, memories, rivals, supply commissions, and equipment progression.

Native powers & beasts

Ninshu provides chakra, jutsu, clans, eyes, mentors, summons, gear and all nine beast mobs. The addon adds field studies and shared combat trials around the installed native mechanics.

A detailed build candidate with clear boundaries. The map is original, compressed Minecraft architecture. Some native jutsu, clan acquisition paths, legendary weapon effects and Jin Bond progression are unfinished upstream. Beast trials award study records and credits; they do not grant jinchuriki status. Read content and limitations and what has actually been tested.

Bring your squad

Everyone imports the same pack. Host: open your world to LAN and share the e4mc address shown in chat. Friends: Multiplayer → Direct Connection. Keep the host's world open. A real session across separate networks still needs acceptance testing.

Optional local AI adds dialogue to your mission contact. It is disabled by default; authored dialogue and quest progress work without it. See NPC and AI setup. No model can change your game state.
