02 / YOUR CHRONICLE

More than a map.

Your mission path, contacts and rivals keep track of what you do. Native Naruto content supplies chakra, jutsu, weapons, mentors and creatures; original systems bring them into a connected campaign.

01

04

Study each beast in the field, then challenge its actual native creature in shared sanctuary trials with friends.

九

TAILED BEAST DOSSIERS

Nine beings.
Nine different histories.

Shukaku, Matatabi, Isobu, Son Goku, Kokuo, Saiken, Chomei, Gyuki and Kurama each have a field study and a native-mob trial. Victory awards mission credit and a record of the encounter. The trials do not grant Jin Bond or make you a jinchuriki.

04 / FIRST STEPS

From import to first mission.

  1. 01

    Import

    Use Prism Launcher’s Add Instance → Import and select the .mrpack. Sign in with your Minecraft Java account and use Java 17.

  2. 02

    Begin

    Create a Survival world, finish Ninshu’s introduction and read its Ninja Guide to learn the native controls and prerequisites.

  3. 03

    Embark

    Use /story to choose your route, then /story embark to enter the shinobi world. /shinobi opens the equipment and beast desk.
