Shinobi field campaigns

Five village allegiances offer 54 chapters each: three home-village induction missions followed by the same 51-chapter story chronology. The rogue route has 14 original chapters. These are 284 route-specific records, not 284 distinct story arcs. The player is an original support shinobi; dialogue and assignments are original fan adaptations. Spoilers cover Naruto and Shippuden through the Fourth Shinobi World War.

What missions actually do

Zabuza and Orochimaru encounters are optional native trials because their real native attributes and jutsu can overwhelm an early character. Completing one adds 12 credits when that chapter is reported; skipping it does not award the bonus. Later Pain, Danzo, White Zetsu and War Obito battles are required. Survival/adventure characters visit generated locations, turn in exact inventory quantities, complete server-checked focus or footwork lessons, and fight explicitly labeled native combat retellings where a real entity exists. A visit is not completion: every listed requirement must be met before reporting beside an owner-bound liaison. Missing reward items fail without spending inventory. Each chapter advances once. Aid gives contact trust and local goodwill; ambition gives village influence and four extra credits. Both choices advance the same authored chronology.

Field titles are add-on achievements. They do not overwrite native rank, clan, bloodline, chakra or jutsu. Lore mentions of unscripted events are explicitly identified; every scene, death, transformation, dimension and canonical ending is not simulated. NPC liaisons and recurring rivals are original characters rendered with vanilla villager/vindicator models.

Commands

/story shows objectives. /story join leaf|sand|mist|cloud|stone|rogue makes a persistent allegiance choice. /story embark provides one initial journey and sets a safe respawn. /story contact calls your liaison to dry ground. /story train starts the current field lesson at its destination, beside your liaison. /story encounter starts the listed native encounter. /story finish aid|ambition reports completion. /story travel <atlas_id> spends 8 base credits, reduced by relationship discounts, to revisit an explored location.

Focus requires 200 consecutive crouched ticks on ground within four blocks of the start. Footwork requires 80 horizontal blocks of movement at the destination, excluding passengers, gliding, water movement and large single-tick displacements. Leaving the destination or changing chapter cancels the lesson; travel cancels it. These lessons do not claim to detect whether a specific native jutsu was cast.
