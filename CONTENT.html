Chakra, ninja tools and the nine tailed beasts

Shinobi Chronicles focuses its campaign on Naruto and Naruto: Shippuden. Ninshu: Origins is the content engine. Its wider catalogue also contains Boruto, anime-only and original fan entries. A registered item is evidence that the item exists, not that all its namesake's abilities are working. The companion compendium lists the installed artifact's actual entries.

Your character and native progression

Complete Ninshu's introduction and read the Ninja Guide supplied on first login. Use Controls → Ninshu to inspect charge chakra, movement, jutsu selection and use keys; use Controlling to find conflicts. The addon does not forcibly rewrite the native clan, nature, village, eyes, chakra, skills or mastery.

Campaign allegiance chooses your mission contact and story perspective. It is separate from Ninshu's native identity and rank. Mission credits buy curated equipment; they are not native Ryo. Advancement through our story is not evidence that a native jutsu was unlocked. Techniques keep their own learner, teacher, resource and mastery requirements.

The installed mod contains learners for the five basic elements—Fire, Wind, Lightning, Earth and Water—along with medical, taijutsu, genjutsu, kenjutsu and ninjutsu branches. It also contains bloodline, clan, ocular and beast branches. The existence of Wood, Ice, Sharingan, Rinnegan, sage or beast content does not make every branch available to every new character. Check the native guide before choosing a long progression path.

There are native mentor entities and interactions. Kakashi's native teaching includes Chidori; Jiraiya's includes Rasengan, summoning and frog sage training. The addon places protected representatives at its fixed training sites, while their native interaction code decides eligibility. Teacher placement is a map adaptation; the addon does not fake successful instruction by giving a renamed vanilla item.

/shinobi beasts opens the field desk. Study at the associated village with /shinobi beasts study <id>. The accepted IDs are shukaku, matatabi, isobu, son_goku, kokuo, saiken, chomei, gyuki, kurama.

After completing 12 story missions, travel to the original Beast Sanctuary at X -550, Z -2350. Use /shinobi beasts trial <id> in Survival. Each trial spawns the actual native beast, with its native model and combat code. Only one trial can occupy the shared sanctuary at a time; its reservation is saved across server restarts. Trials expire after 20 minutes of world time. Eligible nearby allies who deal damage share victory credit. They need that beast's field study and the same minimum mission progression. Report there with /shinobi beasts claim <id> for 40 mission credits and a commemorative study seal. Each character claims each reward once.

The trial is an original combat challenge. Defeating a beast does not canonically earn its trust. The study seal is a commemorative amethyst item, not a container for its chakra. These trials do not seal a beast into the player, advance Ninshu's Jin Bond, or unlock its transformations.

The Ten-Tails is part of the war chronicle, not a tenth member of the nine sibling beasts. The installed artifact has a Juubi learner entry; it does not register a Juubi creature alongside the nine beast entities. No fake Ten-Tails boss is substituted for it.

Canon equipment you can earn

The curated quartermaster supplies actual native items. Standard tools include kunai, shuriken, Fuma shuriken, explosive tags, smoke bombs and healing tags. Weapons are gated by completed story missions and paid for in mission credits. Trust and goodwill reduce prices. The same credit ledger survives death.

All seven named Mist swords are represented by native equipment entries: Kubikiribocho, Samehada, Hiramekarei, Kiba, Nuibari, Kabutowari and Shibuki. Kabutowari's listed item is the hammer component. Special mechanics are limited by the installed mod; a model and attack statistic do not imply every anime ability such as chakra absorption, repair, sewing, paired attacks or explosions.

Other curated equipment includes Kusanagi, Orochimaru's Kusanagi, Gunbai, Temari's fan, Hidan's scythe, Raijin kunai, chakra blades, the White Light Chakra Sabre, Bubblepipe, Demonic Flute, Yagura's staff and Adamantine Staff. Bashosen, Kokinjo, Benihisago and Shichiseiken appear as late shop equipment. The relic names do not promise a complete word-soul sealing puzzle or anime-level power.

Headbands for all five major villages and the Allied Shinobi Forces are available. Search the compendium for the wider native clothing and cosmetics catalogue. The curated shop avoids upstream joke weapons and original fan weapons; these can still appear in the installed mod's general item lists.

Known upstream limits in this pin

The jar's own guide explicitly describes unfinished features. Some release techniques are placeholders. Several clan acquisition paths—including listed Senju/Wood, Yuki/Ice, Kurama-clan and Jugo paths—are incomplete. Kurama clan and Kurama the Nine-Tails are distinct entries. Normal Jin Bond training is not implemented in the inspected version. A cloak toggle checking a bond or mastery value is not proof of a complete transformation progression. The native /sevenswords command is not a complete weapon acquisition system.

The addon provides structured exploration, mission progression, protected mentors, a real equipment economy and beast encounters around that content. It does not claim to repair every unfinished upstream ability. Runtime tests verify registered items and creature construction; individual combat animations, damage balance, teaching chains and high-level forms still need playtesting.
